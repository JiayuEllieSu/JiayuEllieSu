# Jiayu (Ellie) Su

I'm a student at Phillips Academy interested in mathematics, physics, and scientific computing. My work focuses on computational modeling, numerical methods, and data-driven inference, particularly in spatial biology, fluid dynamics, and signal processing.

To learn more about me, check out my **[personal website](https://github.com/JiayuEllieSu/HistoRange)**.

## Selected projects

- **[HistoRange](https://github.com/JiayuEllieSu/HistoRange)**: Histology-informed anisotropic transport and statistical inference for cell-cell signaling in spatial transcriptomics, with safeguards against reporting unidentifiable signaling ranges.
- **[WACCCN](https://github.com/JiayuEllieSu/WACCCN)**: Wavelet-based multiscale, anisotropy-aware cell-cell communication inference and spatial niche segmentation.
- **[Noncommutative Shear Flows](https://github.com/JiayuEllieSu/noncommutative-shear-flows)**: Area-preserving shear-map models of passive tracer advection, material stretching, and the onset of chaotic dynamics.
- **[M-Band Wavelets](https://github.com/JiayuEllieSu/mbandwt)**: Python implementations of multichannel filter banks and wavelet transforms, with perfect-reconstruction validation and a mathematical exposition.
